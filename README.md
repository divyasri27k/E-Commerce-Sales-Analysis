# E-Commerce Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce business performance using Microsoft Power BI. It explores sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance, order status, payment methods, and customer retention.

The goal is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Modelling
- Data Visualization

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Business Analysis:** Overall business performance and key KPIs.
2. **Sales Performance:** Monthly revenue trends, category performance, and city-wise sales.
3. **Customer Analytics:** Customer activity, signups, and purchase behaviour.
4. **Product & Category Analysis:** Top and bottom products and category revenue contribution.
5. **Orders & Payment Analysis:** Order status, payment methods, cancellations, and delivery performance.
6. **Customer Value & Retention:** Repeat purchases, customer value tiers, and retention depth.
7. **Executive Summary:** Key findings, business problems, and recommendations.

## Key Business Findings

Based on the current report:

- Delivered revenue is approximately ₹79.79 million.
- Gross order value across all orders is approximately ₹116.15 million.
- There are 500 total orders.
- The delivery rate is 69.8%.
- Electronics contributes approximately 36.9% of delivered revenue.
- 58 pending orders are more than 90 days old.
- Profit analysis is not available because product cost data is not included.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate pending and cancelled orders to improve fulfilment.
- Review the reasons behind low delivery performance.
- Monitor high-performing product categories and stock levels.
- Improve customer acquisition and retention strategies.
- Include product cost data for future profitability analysis.

## Important Data Notes

Some customer signup dates conflict with order dates. Customer acquisition and repeat-purchase metrics should therefore be interpreted cautiously until the source data is validated.

May 2025 and May 2026 are partial months, so monthly comparisons require care.

## How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file.
2. Open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Explore the seven report pages.
4. Use the slicers to filter the analysis by available business dimensions.

## Project Purpose

This project was developed as a practical Data Analytics portfolio project to demonstrate Power BI dashboard development, data analysis, DAX calculations, visualization, and business insight generation.
